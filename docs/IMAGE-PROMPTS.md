# Derrick Teo — Master Image Prompts (Google Stitch / Imagen / Whisk)

Image-generation prompt kit to populate **derrickteo.com/new/**.
Brand: AI workforce-transformation leader · operator-builder · Singapore. Professional, authoritative, with tasteful AI flavor.

---

## How to use

- **In Stitch:** paste the **STYLE BLOCK** as your project theme/brief, then run each per-section prompt. Stitch is UI-first, so for standalone hero/photo assets these same prompts also work in **Imagen 3 / Whisk / Midjourney / Firefly** as a fallback.
- **Consistency:** keep the STYLE BLOCK identical across every image, fix a single **seed**, and feed your first approved image back in as a *style reference* for the rest.
- **Likeness/authenticity:** don't pass an AI-generated person off as Derrick. Use his **real headshot** for any identity shot; use AI only for backgrounds, conceptual, silhouette, or environmental images.
- **Export:** generate 2× for retina, export WebP/AVIF, keep hero under ~300 KB.

## STYLE BLOCK (prepend or append to EVERY prompt)

> STYLE: editorial corporate-tech, premium and authoritative. Deep near-black navy base (#0D1116) with charcoal (#141A22) surfaces; **electric-cyan (#00D4FF)** primary accent and **muted antique-gold (#D4AF37)** secondary accent, used sparingly. Cinematic volumetric lighting, soft rim light, shallow depth of field. Subtle AI flavor — fine particle constellations, faint holographic data-lines, delicate node-network filaments — integrated tastefully, never cluttered. Generous clean negative space for text. Ultra-detailed, photographic realism or crisp minimal 3D, 8k. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## NEGATIVE / AVOID

> AVOID: garish rainbow neon, cyberpunk clutter, cheesy humanoid robots, glowing-brain or hologram-head clichés, dense circuit-board overload, generic stock-photo handshakes, oversaturation, plastic skin, deformed hands or faces, gibberish lettering, watermarks, logos.

## Per-section prompts

### A. Hero background — 16:9 (also make a 9:16 mobile crop)
> A cinematic, atmospheric backdrop evoking Singapore as a global tech-and-talent hub at blue hour: an abstracted Marina Bay / CBD skyline dissolving on the right into a soft constellation of glowing cyan data-nodes and fine network filaments, as if the city is turning intelligent. Keep the left two-thirds dark and uncluttered for headline text. Layered depth, volumetric haze, a faint gold glow on the horizon. Mood: confident, forward-looking, premium. + STYLE

### B. Founder / executive mood — 4:5 (secondary; composite real headshot for the true portrait)
> A poised senior business leader shown as a three-quarter back view / silhouette (face not clearly visible) standing in a darkened modern Singapore office at night, floor-to-ceiling windows with city-light bokeh, looking toward a faint translucent holographic dashboard of soft cyan workforce and data visualisations. Editorial, restrained, powerful. Shallow depth of field. + STYLE

### C. Three pillar icons — 1:1 (generate as a matched set / same seed)
> A set of three matching minimalist emblem icons on deep navy, thin-line with a soft cyan-to-gold gradient and subtle glow, premium fintech style, centered with generous negative space:
> 1) OPERATOR — a steady hand / classical pillar / ledger fused with a city grid.
> 2) BUILDER — a wrench/code-bracket merging into assembling AI nodes.
> 3) BACKED — a shield/laurel interwoven with a handshake made of light.
> Identical stroke weight, scale and lighting across all three. + STYLE

### D. "The Shift" / story — 1:1 or 4:5
> A conceptual editorial image of transformation: a human hand and a luminous AI "hand" made of cyan particles meeting to assemble glowing modular blocks that resemble HR and operations systems. Warm gold on the human side, cool cyan on the AI side — harmonious, collaborative, not adversarial. Dark cinematic backdrop. + STYLE

### E. The Lab (portfolio) — 16:9
> An elegant dark "command room" tableau: a gently curved array of floating translucent UI panels and dashboards — sales intelligence, retail-shelf vision, a learning platform, job-redesign tooling — rendered as clean abstract glassmorphism with cyan data and small gold highlights, soft reflections on a dark surface. Many working systems, organised and premium, never chaotic. + STYLE

### F. Speaking / keynote — 16:9
> A cinematic wide shot of an authoritative keynote moment: a lone speaker silhouette on a minimalist dark stage, a vast softly-glowing screen behind showing abstract cyan workforce-transformation data, a blurred audience in the foreground. Prestige-conference energy, volumetric spotlight, gold edge light. Face not identifiable. + STYLE

### G. Recognition accent — 16:9 or 3:1 banner
> A refined abstract emblem of achievement: a subtle antique-gold laurel / award motif dissolving into fine cyan particles on deep navy, symmetrical, understated luxury, with large dark negative space. + STYLE

### H. CTA band background — 21:9 (very subtle)
> A mostly-dark premium texture: a faint diagonal light sweep, a sparse cyan node constellation and a single warm gold glow, with plenty of empty space for a centered headline. Low contrast, elegant. + STYLE

### I. OG / social-share card — 1.91:1 (1200×630)
> A premium social-share background: deep navy with an abstracted Singapore skyline-to-network motif on the right, cyan and gold accents, and a strong clean empty area on the left for a name and tagline (text added later). Balanced, professional, scroll-stopping but not busy. + STYLE

### J. Monogram / favicon — 1:1 (optional)
> A minimal monogram combining the letters "DT" into one confident geometric glyph, cyan-to-gold gradient on deep navy, premium tech-brand feel, scalable, centered, generous padding. + STYLE
